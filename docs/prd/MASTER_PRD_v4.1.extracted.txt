2. Executive Product Thesis
Levav is an African workforce intelligence platform that develops, measures and verifies workforce readiness.
Levav is not primarily a job board, social network, assessment website, learning marketplace or gig platform. Those 
surfaces exist to support one connected workforce intelligence system.
The governing principle is Person over Credential.
Qualifications remain useful. They are not enough. Levav must understand what a person can demonstrate, how that 
person behaves in real work contexts, how capability changes over time, what evidence supports each conclusion, and 
what happens after opportunity is given.
The product loop is:
Talent Identity -> Levav 28 -> Observed Behaviour -> Evidence Graph -> WRI -> Development -> Opportunity -> Verified 
Outcome -> Employer Intelligence -> Workforce Intelligence -> Better Levav 28 and WRI.
A feature is core only if it does at least one of these things:
• Develops readiness.
• Proves readiness.
• Creates or improves meaningful opportunity.
• Reduces employer uncertainty.
• Creates verified evidence.
• Improves workforce decisions.
• Learns from real outcomes.
3. The African Problem Levav Must Solve
3.1 Talent problem
Millions of young Africans enter the labour market while the supply of formal wage employment remains far smaller than 
the number of people seeking it.
The product must respond to two separate problems:
• A shortage of formal opportunities.
• A readiness and evidence gap that makes employers uncertain about who can perform well.
Levav must not teach a graduate to sit still until a perfect permanent vacancy appears.
It should help people remain economically and professionally active. A person should be able to develop capability, 
demonstrate it, offer productive capacity, build verified evidence and become more visible for the right opportunities.
3.2 Startup and SME problem
Many startups and growing organisations need work completed before they can afford full-time payroll.
They may need five hours of bookkeeping, two weeks of customer research, a three-month marketing coordinator, a 
fractional finance lead, a landing page, a reconciliation exercise, field data collection or operational support.
Levav must make this demand usable. QuickWork turns work that is too small, too early, too irregular or too specialised 
for a permanent hire into structured opportunity.
3.3 Employer problem
CVs and interviews can hide whether a candidate can think, prioritise, communicate, learn, collaborate and execute.
Levav replaces hiring by guesswork with evidence-first decision support.
3.4 Workforce institution problem
Traditional labour data can show participation, qualifications and employment status without showing demonstrated 
readiness, capability gaps, evidence quality or development trajectory.

12. Workforce Readiness Index Scientific Methodology
WRI is a versioned interpretation layer over evidence.
It is not a personality score, intelligence score, social popularity score or guaranteed predictor of employment success.
12.1 Core dimensions
1. Critical Thinking: Analyses information, tests assumptions, evaluates alternatives and reaches reasoned conclusions.
2. Problem Solving: Defines problems, identifies causes, develops options, implements solutions and evaluates results.
3. Initiative and Ownership: Identifies useful work, acts appropriately, accepts responsibility and follows through.
4. Reliability and Execution: Fulfils commitments, manages deadlines, communicates delays and delivers.
5. Communication: Listens, asks useful questions and communicates clearly in relevant professional contexts.
6. Collaboration and Teamwork: Coordinates with others, manages disagreement and supports group outcomes.
7. Adaptability and Learning Agility: Responds to change, feedback and unfamiliar situations and improves over time.
8. Professional Discipline: Demonstrates preparation, time management, documentation, quality control and conduct.
9. Leadership Readiness: Demonstrates decision judgement, delegation, accountability, conflict handling and development of 
others at an appropriate level.
10. Contribution and Service Orientation: Creates verified value through contribution, support, service or mentorship beyond 
narrow self-interest.
12.2 Required WRI outputs
WRI-001: Store and present the following separately:
• Overall WRI estimate.
• Dimension profile.
• Evidence confidence.
• Evidence coverage.
• Readiness trajectory.
• Role Readiness where a target role exists.
WRI-002: WRI and Role Fit are separate.
A person may have strong general workforce readiness and weak fit for one role. Role Fit uses role requirements, 
relevant evidence, skills and Workforce Graph context. WRI describes demonstrated general readiness.
WRI-003: Confidence is separate from performance.
A high score with weak evidence must look different from a high score supported by diverse, recent evidence.
Confidence may increase while a score falls if stronger new evidence reveals a weakness.
Confidence inputs may include:
• Observation count.
• Evidence diversity.
• Independent sources.
• E3 and E4 coverage.

12.3 Scientific validation programme
WRI must be treated as an evolving measurement system.
Required validation questions include:
• Content validity: Do tasks represent real work behaviour for the role and level?
• Construct validity: Does a dimension measure the intended behaviour rather than unrelated factors?
• Criterion validity: Does the measure relate to meaningful outcomes such as completion, rehire, probation, performance or 
retention?
• Test-retest reliability: Are estimates reasonably stable when underlying capability has not changed?
• Inter-rater reliability: Do qualified raters reach similar conclusions from the same evidence?
• Scenario equivalence: Are alternate scenarios comparable in difficulty and construct coverage?
• Fairness: Are outcomes systematically disadvantaging groups without job-related justification?
• Calibration: Do readiness levels correspond to observed outcomes over time?
12.4 Scientific governance
WRI model changes require governed approval.
The governance structure should include product leadership and qualified expertise in industrial-organisational 
psychology, psychometrics, data science, labour economics, HR, data protection and relevant sector domains.
WRI-GOV-001:
• Construct definitions are documented.
• Major scoring changes are versioned.
• Historical cohort tests run before release.
• Fairness analysis is part of model release review.
• Users have a dispute and review route for evidence that materially affects readiness conclusions.
