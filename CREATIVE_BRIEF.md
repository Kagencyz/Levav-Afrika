# LEVAV™ HOMEPAGE — CREATIVE EXPERIENCE BRIEF

## Philosophy
The homepage is not a landing page. It is a cinematic journey through the soul of Levav™.
Each moment answers a fundamental question. Each moment creates an emotional response.
The visitor should not just understand Levav™ — they should FEEL it.

## The Five Questions → Five Moments

### MOMENT 1: "WHY" — The Hero (Why does Levav™ exist?)
**Question:** Why does Levav™ exist?
**Emotion:** Awe. Possibility. The grandeur of the vision.
**Visual:** Full-viewport cinematic hero. Slow-moving gradient aurora (black → violet → lime) as background. Large floating glass panels with key statistics. A single powerful headline: "Africa's Workforce Intelligence Ecosystem™" with the tagline "Transforming Potential into Capability" in gradient text.
**Motion:** Particles floating upward. Glass panels drifting slowly. Text reveals with staggered fade-up. Parallax on scroll.
**Interaction:** Mouse movement subtly shifts the aurora gradient. Scroll indicator pulses.
**Layout:** 100vh. No scroll needed to understand the mission.

### MOMENT 2: "HOW IT WORKS" — The Transformation Journey (How does Levav™ work?)
**Question:** How does Levav™ work?
**Emotion:** Clarity. Understanding. The elegance of the system.
**Visual:** A horizontal/vertical scrolling transformation pathway. Six glowing nodes connected by an animated gradient line: Potential → Capability → Contribution → Opportunity → Prosperity → Development. Each node reveals content on hover/click.
**Motion:** The connecting line draws itself as you scroll (scroll-driven SVG animation). Each node pulses when reached. Content fades in beside active node.
**Interaction:** Click any node to expand details. The line glows brighter near the active node.
**Layout:** Sticky scroll section — left side shows the pathway, right side reveals content.

### MOMENT 3: "DIFFERENT" — The Ecosystem Reveal (Why is Levav™ different?)
**Question:** Why is Levav™ different?
**Emotion:** Intelligence. Sophistication. Trust in the system.
**Visual:** Four large glass ecosystem cards arranged in a bento-style grid: Levav 28™ (Transformation), QuickWork™ (Activation), Levav Impact™ (Contribution), WRI™ (Intelligence). Each card has a unique ambient animation (floating orbs, pulsing rings, etc.).
**Motion:** Cards float in from different directions. On hover, each card expands slightly and reveals more detail. Subtle ambient animations inside each card.
**Interaction:** Hover expands card. Click opens modal with full ecosystem detail.
**Layout:** 2x2 grid on desktop, stacked on mobile. Each card ~45vh.

### MOMENT 4: "LIVES" — The Human Stories (How does Levav™ change lives?)
**Question:** How does Levav™ change lives?
**Emotion:** Connection. Hope. Real impact.
**Visual:** Three featured talent/story cards with real photography. Each card shows: photo, name, location, transformation journey (before → after), quote. Cards are large and immersive.
**Motion:** Cards slide in from bottom with stagger. Photos have subtle zoom on hover. Quote text reveals character by character.
**Interaction:** Click card to expand full story. Hover triggers a "ripple" effect on the photo.
**Layout:** Horizontal scroll or 3-column grid. Full-bleed imagery.

### MOMENT 5: "AFRICA" — The Vision & CTA (How does Levav™ strengthen Africa?)
**Question:** How does Levav™ strengthen Africa?
**Emotion:** Belonging. Purpose. The call to action.
**Visual:** A panoramic map visualization of Africa with glowing connection points (cities where Levav™ operates). Text overlay: "Building for Africa. Building for Generations." Final CTA section with two buttons.
**Motion:** Map connection points pulse sequentially. Lines connect between cities. Counter animates: "45+ Countries" "2,500+ Talents Activated" "98% Completion Rate".
**Interaction:** Hover over map points to see city names. Click CTA to begin journey.
**Layout:** Full-width. Glass gradient background. CTA buttons glow on hover.

## Design Language
- **Liquid Glass:** Every surface is frosted glass with backdrop-blur
- **Ambient Motion:** Nothing is static — everything breathes, floats, or pulses
- **Gradient Energy:** Lime → Violet transitions throughout
- **Generous Spacing:** Premium feel through whitespace and breathing room
- **Real Photography:** African professionals, builders, innovators — NOT stock
- **Typography:** Geist for headlines (large, bold), Inter for body (15-16px minimum)

## Technical Approach
- Framer Motion for all scroll-driven and hover animations
- CSS backdrop-filter for liquid glass effects
- SVG animations for the transformation pathway
- Intersection Observer for scroll-triggered reveals
- Real Unsplash images for talent photography
- Particle system for ambient background motion
